Privacy Policy

Yalla Bola · version 2026-08-terms-v2 · last updated 21 August 2026

You can use Yalla Bola without an account, and then it collects nothing at all. No sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, nothing sent anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your phone. That mode is permanent and it is not a trial.

If you choose to create an account, we hold your email address, the name you pick, and a record of the terms you accepted.

Cloud backup is a separate choice, and it is off until you turn it on. With it on, a copy of your games is stored on our servers so a new phone can get them back. With it off — which is how it starts — your games never leave the device.

Who is responsible

Yalla Bola is published by Amr Khalifa. Questions about this policy, or about the data held about you, go to amr.khalifa@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

To keep score, the app saves the following in a private database on your phone:

  • The player names you type in
  • Your games — bids, tricks won, trumps and the resulting scores
  • Your rule settings and app preferences

None of it is transmitted, backed up to us, or seen by anyone else. There is nothing on a server to ask about, because nothing was sent to one.

What an account holds

An account is optional. If you create one, this is the complete list of what is stored on our servers — there is nothing else.

  • Email address — to identify your account and let you sign back in or reset a password. You type it, or Google supplies it.
  • Display name — the name shown on your profile. You type it, or Google supplies it.
  • Avatar image reference — to draw your profile mark. From Google, if you sign in that way.
  • Language preference — to show the app in the language you chose.
  • Consent record — which version of these documents you accepted, when, that you confirmed you are 16 or over, and whether you asked for product emails.
  • Password — stored only as a cryptographic hash. Nobody, including us, can read it back.

Cloud backup, if you turn it on

Backup is off by default. Nothing about your games leaves the phone until you switch it on in Account, and switching it off again stops any further copies being sent.

With it on, we store:

  • Your games — the players, the bids, the tricks won, the trumps and the rules each game was played under. Scores themselves are not stored; they are worked out from those inputs, which is why fixing a rule repairs old games rather than rewriting them.
  • Your player list and your rule presets, so a new phone is not an empty one.

This exists for one reason: so that losing your phone does not lose your history. It is not analysed, not profiled, and not shared. Deleting your account deletes all of it (see below), and deleting a game deletes it from the server too.

What the app still does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or behavioural tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No access to your contacts or address book — the app never asks, and never will. Invitations are ordinary links you send yourself
  • No matching of your contacts against other people's accounts
  • No location, camera, microphone or file access
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing of it with advertisers
  • No third-party tracking software of any kind

Who processes it for us

Two companies handle data on our behalf, and only to make the account work.

  • Supabase hosts the account database and the sign-in service. Data is stored on their infrastructure, which is outside Egypt.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google then tells us your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture link. Google's own handling of your data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Neither of them is permitted to use your data for their own purposes through us.

Sharing a result

The app can pass a plain-text summary of a finished game to your phone's own share sheet, and only when you tap the share button. What happens next is handled by whichever app you choose to send it to, under that app's own privacy policy. Yalla Bola does not keep a copy or send it anywhere itself.

How long it is kept

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. Delete the account and it is deleted immediately — there is no grace period and no archived copy. Data on your phone lasts until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account → Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm who you are, shown exactly what goes and what stays, and then it happens immediately. There is no grace period and no archived copy — the account, the profile, the consent record and any backed-up games are removed outright.

Without the app: if you have uninstalled it, email amr.khalifa@gmail.com from the address on the account, or use the deletion request form linked from the store listing.

Deleting the account does not delete the games on your phone. Those are yours, they stay where they are, and the scorekeeper carries on working as a guest.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it to be corrected, withdraw a consent you gave, or have the whole account deleted. Write to amr.khalifa@gmail.com and you will get an answer within 30 days. You can change your name, language and email preferences yourself in the app at any time.

Children

Using the app without an account is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone. An account requires you to be 16 or older. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, write to us and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the version identifier at the top changes with it, and the app will ask you to read and accept the new version the next time you sign in. Smaller clarifications are posted with a new date.
