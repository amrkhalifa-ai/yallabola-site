Delete your Yalla Bola account

Two ways, and both delete the same things.

In the app is fastest. Open Account → Delete my account. You are asked to confirm who you are, shown exactly what goes and what stays, and then it happens immediately — there is no grace period and no archived copy.

If you no longer have the app

Email amr.khalifa@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the word delete in the subject. We will confirm and delete within 30 days, and usually within a few days.

Write from the account's own email address. It is the only way we can tell it is you asking, and we will not delete somebody's account on a stranger's say-so.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your profile: display name, language, and your photograph, which is removed from storage as well as from the profile
  • Your consent record
  • Any games backed up from your phone, and your player list and rule presets
  • Your device tokens, so notifications stop
  • Your ratings and their history
  • Your seats, requests and attestations
  • Blocks you made, and reports you filed

What is not deleted, and why

  • The games on your phone. Those are yours and they stay where they are. The scorekeeper carries on working as a guest — deleting the account does not delete your history from the device. Uninstalling the app does.
  • Bolas other people played in keep their results, with your name removed. Three other people's histories should not be rewritten by your leaving.
  • Moderation records keep the fact that a decision was made, without the name of the person it was about.

Getting a copy first

Deletion cannot be undone, so if you want your data, take it before you delete: Account → Export my data writes one file containing everything held about you, on the phone and on the server. Or ask at the address above.
